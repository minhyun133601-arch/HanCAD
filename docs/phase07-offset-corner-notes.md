## Implemented geometry

Polyline offsets execute the actual Rust/WASM Cavalier offset algorithm, with self-intersection handling and multiple output loops. Each source is translated to its first vertex before transfer to keep absolute survey coordinates out of the kernel's geometric comparisons. Bulges greater than a semicircle split before entry. Gap 0 replaces generated corner rounds using intersections of the neighboring supporting lines/circles, recalculating trimmed arc bulges; gap 2 replaces only generated rounds with straight connectors. Original source arcs remain arcs.

Elliptical and regular NURBS parallels use adaptive piecewise cubic Bezier fitting, stored as a real degree 3 SPLINE with a documented **1e-6 mm** sampled residual target. A generic ellipse parallel is not a rational conic, so this result is explicitly numerical. Curvature cusps trigger a topology pass: regular spans that maintain the specified clearance from the source survive, while inward loops are removed. Connected surviving spans combine as one spline; disconnected spans return separately. The [ObjectARX trimmed-offset contract](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2019/ENU/OARX-RefGuide/files/OREF-AcGeCurve3d__getTrimmedOffset_double_AcGeVector3d__AcGeVoidPointerArray__AcGe__OffsetCrvExtType_AcGeTol__const.html) documents removal of self-intersecting loops and connected-component outputs. The numerical trimming policy is an implementation inference from that contract; installed AutoCAD must verify its topology choices. Singular source tangents remain a localized failure.

The topology fallback prepares at most 16,384 adaptive source spans, refines at most eight local candidates per nearest-point query, limits classification to 4,096 calls and uses at most 512 initial topology samples over at most 128 knot spans. Excessive complexity fails explicitly. Fixed-distance numerical-curve previews cache at most eight source/side results within the command; accepted copies always receive fresh identities. The measured cusp test decreased from roughly 0.7 seconds to roughly 0.15 seconds after local nearest-point refinement. This bounds the fallback; it does not certify the typical-edit 16 ms budget for pathological splines.

FILLET uses analytic offset-support intersections for lines, rays/xlines, arcs and circles. CHAMFER constructs exact distance/angle intersections for straight supports. Adjacent or one-segment-separated selections within one polyline preserve its identity. Separate line/polyline combinations, including a picked interior polyline segment, merge into one native polyline. The selected side and its connected path survive trimming; the opposite branch is removed. This branch policy is an explicit installed-AutoCAD comparison item. Selecting the exposed first/last segments of a longer open polyline closes the path with the fillet. The Polyline option adds corners or removes/replaces previous fillet arcs; No Trim creates independent connectors. Changed vertex counts also remap per-vertex widths, fixing a browser-discovered commit rejection for PLINE's explicit zero-width arrays.

The parallel-line exception applies even when remembered FILLETRAD is zero: the connector uses half the separation. A parallel construction requires a LINE or RAY as the first object, following the official reference. Trimmed XLINE supports become exact RAYs; a trimmed RAY becomes either a new RAY or a finite LINE according to the selected side. The original infinite geometry is replaced rather than left underneath an apparently successful connector.

The final option audit reproduced a nonzero-elevation loss: a polyline at Z=7 retained its legacy elevation field while generated point/curve vertices moved to Z=0. OFFSET, FILLET and CHAMFER now preserve the original 2D plane in both point and exact-curve representations. Independent LINE offset/corner constructions also preserve their endpoint Z coordinates. Three new regressions cover the real WASM gap variants, both corner commands, separate lines and elevation-only polyline storage.

BLEND uses the endpoint picked on each open curve. Its endpoint tangent speed is the chord length between the selected ends. Smooth's quintic controls enforce the normal second derivative from source endpoint curvature; finite differences estimate source second derivatives. Autodesk documents continuity and degree but not its control-point/tension convention, so exact installed-AutoCAD shape identity remains a comparison item.
